# The Lineup — Site Spec
**Client:** Joshua Kevin R. Bondoc  
**Type:** Editorial portfolio / resume-magazine hybrid  
**Location:** Vancouver, BC  
**Version:** 0.1 — Concept draft
 
---
 
## Concept
 
A publication-first personal site where Joshua's professional experience is presented as editorial content, not a job application. Every section maps to a magazine convention: case studies are features, his CV lives on the masthead, board sports writing fills the columns and field notes. The site is genuinely dual-purpose — a portfolio for potential employers and a real publication for readers interested in content strategy and Pacific Northwest board sports.
 
**Name:** The Lineup  
*In surfing, the lineup is where you read conditions and position yourself for the next wave. In publishing, it's your queue of content. The name works in both directions without forcing the metaphor.*
 
**Tagline:** Content · Sustainability · Board sports
